# Medical disclaimer

RetinaTriage AI is an investigational software prototype for technical research and education. It is not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, emergency service, certified medical device, or replacement for a qualified eye-care professional.

Automated outputs may be wrong, poorly calibrated, affected by image quality, or unreliable outside the development population and acquisition setting. A passing quality gate does not guarantee clinical adequacy. A low-risk output does not exclude disease.

The configured composite image-quality acceptance threshold is 75/100. Scores below 75 cause the disease grade to be withheld and require retake or manual review. A score of 75 or above passes only this transparent software heuristic. The threshold is not clinically validated and does not guarantee clinical gradability, diagnostic accuracy, or suitability for patient care.

Grad-CAM highlights regions that influenced model output. It is not lesion segmentation, clinical annotation, proof of pathology or a causal explanation.

An ophthalmologist must assess the original image, patient context and all relevant clinical evidence and make the final decision.
