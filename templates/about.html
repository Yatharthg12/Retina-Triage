RETINATRIAGE AI

Earlier review for the cases that may need it most.

A research platform for image-quality assessment, five-grade diabetic-retinopathy screening, uncertainty routing, visual explanation and auditable prioritization.

01

Quality gate

Blur, exposure, contrast, resolution and retinal-field coverage are checked before a disease output is displayed.

02

Severity screening

An EfficientNetB0 demonstration model estimates grade 0–4 probabilities, referable DR and high-risk DR; its confidence is explicitly marked uncalibrated.

03

Human review

Severe, poor-quality or uncertain cases are escalated in a deterministic priority service.

Intended research use

  • Retrospective validation and workflow research
  • Screening queue prioritization under clinician oversight
  • Education using properly governed, de-identified data

Prohibited use

  • Autonomous diagnosis or treatment decisions
  • Emergency assessment or exclusion of disease
  • Use as a certified medical device
Medical disclaimer. Every image and output must be reviewed in the appropriate clinical context by a qualified ophthalmologist. Grad-CAM is not lesion segmentation, clinical annotation or proof of pathology.
Quality-threshold disclaimer. The configured acceptance threshold is 75/100. Scores below 75 require retake/manual review; scores of 75 or above pass only an unvalidated software heuristic and do not guarantee clinical gradability or diagnostic reliability.
